# QA

Three kinds of check ran, at three times: level 0 QA of the whole store
before anything was exported, stripe scoring of every pyramid, and
per-tile validation off the store as rebuilds landed.

## Level 0, before export

`qa_level0.py` reads every array of every land tile-year once (858 x 7
arrays, 1.6 minutes at eight workers) and counts. Results per tile-year
and per year go to `stats/qa_level0.json`; `--summary` prints the table
and lists the worst tile-years. On the finished v1 store (2026-09-06):

Completeness. All 858 land tile-years have data and the 16 water tiles
are empty in every year. Valid pixels are 99.4 to 100 percent of the
land-tile area per year (water is kept).

Nodata consistency. count == 0 if and only if all six bands == 0,
exactly, in every tile-year. No band-zero-with-count pixels at all,
because the runner requires every band valid for a look, so the USGS
unflagged cloud-edge nodata case does not occur in the composite.

Reflectance below 0.0 (DN under 7273) over valid pixels: nir 3.0
percent, swir1 and swir2 0.8, red 0.6, blue and green under 0.02.
Concentrated on the ocean tiles, 10 to 11 percent of valid pixels
summed over bands on r04c03, r06c02 and r06c03: aerosol overcorrection
over water, the documented Collection 2 behaviour. It rises after 2013
(0.9 to 1.8 percent per year, all bands) from 0.2 to 0.6 before,
Landsat 8 and 9's LaSRC against LEDAPS. Not a defect; the product README
says so.

Above 1.0 (DN over 43636): 0.000 to 0.002 percent per year. Every
value above the Collection 2 valid range (65455) is exactly 65535,
saturation: 127k pixels in blue, 102k red, 64k green, 26k swir1, 9k
swir2, 2k nir over all 26 years, most in 2012 (47k) and 2003 (38k),
none in 2024 or 2025.

clear_count per year: mean 6.6 to 11.4 looks; 2000, 2003 and 2012 were
the thin years before the rebuilds.

Verdict: nothing to rerun. The v3 and v4 rebuilds changed the look
sets, not the mask or the band validity rule, so these findings hold
for the shipped store; the count statistics moved as docs/03 records.

## The stripe score

`stripe_score.py` (docs/03) runs on every pyramid refresh, 3.5 to 13
minutes, and writes `stats/striping/<year>.json` per block,
`summary.json`, a gated map per year and a raw one. The v3 scores that
the validators compare against are kept in `data/striping_v3/`
(`STRIPE_BASELINE` overrides the path); the pre-fill v1 summary is
`stats/striping/summary_before_l7fill.json`.

## Mid-build validation

The v4 rebuild ran for 30 hours over two batches, and the checks ran
beside it on read-only Icechunk sessions rather than after it.

`validate_tile.py TILE YEAR` scores one rebuilt tile-year off the store
against the v3 tag and the v3 pyramid scores, and prints one line: OK,
or FLAG with the numbers. The rules: gated nir up by more than 1.0 on a
land block; blocks with `cc_ratio` over 5 counted on land only; the
tile's land nir median up; pixels under three looks, compared only over
pixels valid in both versions, with coverage gained reported
separately; nodata fraction up; a seam signature (sector angle 15 to 25
degrees with nir up). Water blocks are excluded from the rules because
a near-zero NIR denominator flags landscape.

`check_rebuilt.py YEAR LOG` scores a whole year off the store and
compares the blocks inside the rebuilt tiles against the v3 scores,
land and water separately, with the blocks outside as the control that
should not move.

`v4_monitor.sh` polled the batch logs every minute, ran
`validate_tile.py` on each tile-year as its "done in" line appeared,
ran `check_rebuilt.py` as each year completed, and printed one event
line per finding (TILE, YEAR_DONE, FAIL, CHAIN, DEAD, HEARTBEAT, END),
with OK lines going to `data/logs/validate.nbr3.log` only. Over the run
it validated every landed tile-year and flagged 9 of the first 122 and
a handful more after, none acted on: one seam (r00c05 2014), three
water-only tiles, and the rest the "nir median up with the count
stripes cleared" shape where a rebuilt tile's terrain texture became
visible once the stripes went. Every year's aggregate was clean on land
(blocks with count stripes down in all 21 years, nir medians down or
flat, outside blocks unchanged) before `after_batch.sh` was allowed to
refresh the pyramid.

## After the export

`export_zarr.py --verify` proves the pyramid's level 0 is the tagged
snapshot, `pyramid.py --verify` proves the levels are the exact mean of
that level 0, and `remote_check.py` reads the uploaded product back
through data.source.coop and compares chunks at every level and
tile-year regions of the store to disk (docs/07). All three ran clean on
what shipped.
